One-click setup

Run START-TITAN from the repository after installing Android Platform Tools. Cell Monitor stays on 127.0.0.1; it never needs your USB-connected phone exposed to the internet.

1
Connect your Android phone by USB.
Keep the phone unlocked during first pairing.
2
Enable USB debugging.
Android: Developer options → USB debugging. Android 16 also supports Wireless debugging, but this one-click path is USB-first.
3
Accept the RSA authorization prompt.
The phone must explicitly trust this computer before ADB reports it as device.
4
Click Discover ADB.
Cell Monitor registers only devices actually visible through ADB.

Limitations

Cell Monitor reports telemetry observed from devices you control. It does not by itself establish interception, malicious activity, ownership of third-party signals, or causation. ADB collection depends on device support and Android permissions. Evidence hashing provides integrity, not independent origin authentication.
